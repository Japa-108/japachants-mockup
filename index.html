Plan v4 · what changed

Chants under Mantras. Puja-ready menu. Same real design.

Placement Inside Mantras tab Chants becomes the 3rd segment — alongside Mantras and Rituals. Your mental model, not a dedicated stack.
Menu redesign Community → Profile, Puja in Community folds into Profile (feed + awards + stats). Puja takes the 4th visible tab slot so it's discoverable when it launches.
Visual language Faithful to the real app White nav with #DEDEDE top border, saffron outlined icons, raised Start Japa circle in the center, underline-only Mantras segments.
Two reading modes 📘 Study · ✦ Chant Kept from v3. Bottom-sheet picker on first open of each chant. Remembered per-chant.
